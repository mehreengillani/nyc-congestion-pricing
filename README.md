
# Is Congestion Pricing Working? Manhattan Traffic After the Toll

In January 2025, New York City became the first US city to charge drivers for entering
its busiest area. One year later, is it working?

This project analyzes the MTA's open data on vehicles entering Manhattan's Congestion
Relief Zone (south of 60th Street), using 325+ million vehicle entries recorded in 10-minute intervals.

**Key findings so far:**
- 🚗 Traffic is **4% lower** in 2026 than in 2025 (Jan 5 – Oct 31)
- 🔻 Private cars are **down 7.3%**, while 🔺 taxis, Uber & Lyft are **up 1.6%**
- 🚕 Ride-hail now makes up **38%** of all entries into the zone
- ❄️ The Feb 2026 blizzard travel ban cut entries by **87%** in one day

📊 Data: [MTA Congestion Relief Zone Vehicle Entries](https://data.ny.gov/d/t6yz-b64h) (NY Open Data)
